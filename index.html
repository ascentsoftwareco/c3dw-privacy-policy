Privacy Policy for Custom 3D Wallpaper

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Custom 3D Wallpaper ("the app") is a live wallpaper app developed by Ascent Software Co. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Summary

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data ourselves. The app displays ads through Google AdMob, which processes some device and usage data on Google's servers to serve and measure those ads, as described below. The app also downloads optional online content we host — news cards, 3D models, sky/lighting presets, and background images — through anonymous file requests that are not tied to any account or personal identifier. Your imported images, 3D models, and wallpaper settings stay on your device unless you choose to share a screenshot of your wallpaper.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device information, and ad interactions. This data is handled by Google, not by us — see Google's Partner Sites policy and Privacy Policy for details on how it's used.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings > Manage Ad Consent in the app.

You can also limit ad personalization on Android by going to Settings > Privacy > Ads and resetting or deleting your advertising ID.

Online content

Some features load optional content that we host on Google Firebase Storage:

  • News cards on the Home screen, which announce app updates and features. These are fetched when the app starts.
  • Curated 3D models on the Discover screen.
  • Sky/lighting presets and background images in the wallpaper editor, including their preview thumbnails.

To show this content, your device makes anonymous requests to Firebase Storage for a list of available items and for any thumbnails or files you view or download. These are plain file downloads — no account, name, email, or other personal identifier is attached. As with any internet request, our hosting provider's infrastructure may log standard connection data (such as IP address and timestamp) to operate the service; we do not access or use this beyond what's needed to serve the files. Downloaded files are stored locally on your device like anything else you import.

Dismissing a news card is remembered on your device only. If a card contains a link and you tap it, the link opens in your web browser and is governed by that website's own privacy policy.

Permissions and device features

  • Choosing images and 3D model files — the app uses Android's system file and photo picker, so it does not need permission to read your photo library. It can only access the specific files you choose. Selected files are copied into the app's private storage on your device so your wallpaper keeps working, and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer/gyroscope) — used to drive the parallax tilt effect on your live wallpaper. This data is processed in real time on-device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. No permission is required.
  • Internet / network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used to request and display ads through Google AdMob, and to fetch the optional online content described above.

Data storage

Your imported images, 3D models, wallpaper presets, app settings, and anything downloaded from our online content are stored locally on your device only (using local app storage and an on-device database). We have no access to this data. It is excluded from device backups, and uninstalling the app removes it.

If you use the app's Share feature, the app creates a screenshot of your wallpaper and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share sheet (for example, a messaging or social app). What happens to it after that is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Third parties

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform — advertising and ad consent, as described above.
  • Google Firebase Storage — hosting for the optional online content described above.
  • Google Play In-App Review — the app may occasionally show Google Play's built-in prompt asking you to rate the app. This is handled by Google Play; we do not receive your rating or any information about you.

We do not integrate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not sell or share any data ourselves.

Your choices and data deletion

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, there is no account or user data held by us to access, correct, or delete. You can delete everything the app stores on your device by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. To control data processed by Google for advertising, use the in-app Manage Ad Consent option (where available), your device's ad settings, or Google's controls described in its Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child's data has been processed through the app's ads, contact us using the details below and we will help however we can.
